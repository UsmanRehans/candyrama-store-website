# Owner collaboration preferences

## Confirmed — September 14, 2026

Source: owner explicitly asked for agentic employees to talk directly, ask questions, become more in tune through collaboration, and work together on future websites.

- Make specialists visible; do not hide all recommendations behind the coordinator.
- Ask focused, useful questions directly and let the owner shape the work.
- Preserve lessons from answers and feedback so later work starts with context.
- Reuse the team across future websites, keeping each brand's identity separate.

## Observed preference — September 15, 2026

For product-led ecommerce homepages, the owner values seeing a broad selection early, as demonstrated by their positive reaction to SweetyTreaty's visible product assortment. Treat this as a starting hypothesis for future stores, then adapt it to catalog size and the new brand's customer journey.

## Scope of learning

These are working preferences, not global aesthetic rules. Candy Rama's small-Texas-team story, catalog, colors and typography feedback belong to its project memory. Silence is not an accepted design decision. Record evidence and user corrections rather than inferred personality profiles.
