## 1. Color in motion

Purpose: an appetizing introduction to an existing gummy product.

Reference: the approved current product image and an actual candy reference for the selected SKU. Vita must inspect these before generation.

Draft generation prompt: Create a six-second portrait candy advertising concept. A small quantity of the exact gummy candy shown in the supplied product reference tumbles gently into a shallow cream ceramic bowl on a warm yellow tabletop. Low, close camera angle, soft studio light, appetizing surface detail, believable gravity and contact shadows. Preserve reference candy shapes, colors and coatings. One controlled camera push, one continuous shot. Keep the central action crop-safe. No packaging, people, hands, writing, logos, additional candy types or implied quantity promise. End on a calm composed bowl suitable for a poster frame.

Postproduction: short Candy Rama title and an optional link to the exact existing product. Label as AI-generated concept in the preview. Reject melting shapes, floating candy, impossible collisions and incorrect product appearance.

## 2. Pink pouch spotlight

Purpose: recognizable brand and packaging, without pretending to show the exact contents of a purchase.

Reference: approved front-facing pink pouch image; retain this asset as an unchanged composited foreground layer wherever possible.

Draft background generation prompt: Create an eight-second portrait studio background for a candy package advertisement. A clean warm cream platform against a soft pink and yellow backdrop, with very subtle moving light and a slow gentle camera drift. Leave an uncluttered central area for a package to be composited later. Soft realistic shadows and a cheerful tactile studio atmosphere. No objects, candy, people, letters, logos or packaging. Keep the platform stable and geometry consistent throughout.

Postproduction: composite the approved Candy Rama pouch without regenerating its lettering or changing its proportions; restrained scale motion and matching contact shadow. Use “Taste the Twist” only as clean editable text or approved artwork. Reject label distortion and movement that falsely suggests an actual product demonstration.

## 3. Movie-night candy moment

Purpose: an illustrative occasion for enjoying current products.

Draft generation prompt: Create an eight-second portrait candy lifestyle advertising concept. A cozy movie-night tabletop with one cream bowl containing only the exact candy shown in the supplied reference. A soft sofa and subtle out-of-focus screen glow in the background. Warm inviting light, gentle camera move toward the bowl, realistic texture and restrained depth of field. No people, faces, hands, readable screen content, brand marks, text or extra product varieties. Keep the bowl and candy stable and recognizable. End in a calm hero composition with space for a short caption.

Postproduction: optional unchanged approved pouch composite only if its perspective and lighting fit. Suggested caption: “Pick your movie-night candy.” Link to the appropriate existing product or catalog. Do not label it a real customer's moment.
