## What should change for Candy Rama

Our interpretation: the useful pattern is **a different shopping question per module**. A new slogan on the same pouch scene adds little information. Preserve the recognizable smooth pink pouch, but give subsequent modules new evidence:

- Identity hero: exact SKU/pouch and a clear candy description.
- Contents: recognizable actual pieces, with flavor names only after SKU confirmation. Do not invent a twelve-flavor grid to imitate the reference.
- Texture: actual coating/interior detail, close enough to understand the bite.
- Pack facts: approved net weight and what the purchase includes; do not imply two campaign pouches form a bundle.
- Occasion: a distinct, believable use image; avoid implying an unsupported yield or serving count.
- Store discovery: distinct category imagery leading to the relevant assortment; A+ remains tied to its product.

Do not copy competitor compositions, logos, wording, unsupported superiority or ingredient claims. The pink pouch remains an identity cue, not the subject of every panel. A short accurate set is preferable to additional panels that repeat the hero. Validate this as a comprehension hypothesis first; competitor creative alone cannot establish a winning sales strategy.
