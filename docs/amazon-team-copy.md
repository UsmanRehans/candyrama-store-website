## Product-specific copy to complete after confirmation

Use this writing pattern: **[approved flavor] + [accurate texture] + [one natural occasion].** For example, only after approval: “[Flavor] with a [texture] bite. Ready for your next movie night.” These brackets are internal brief fields and must never appear in customer artwork.

Missing inputs: target marketplace and ASIN; exact SKU name; included candy/flavor list; pack count and net weight; approved label and product photographs; any texture/coating claims; Amazon module selections. Ingredients, allergens, certifications, origin/manufacturing, price advantage, and inventory must not be inferred from existing website text or generated art. We do not need those claims to produce this restrained review direction.

## Brand review gates

- Packaging stays pink and faithful to the approved source; no rebrand story, invented labels, or competing-brand imagery.
- Headline, pouch name, and candy remain legible at mobile size; crop review must include the selected Amazon builder preview.
- A shopper can tell which product is offered; other products cannot read as included contents.
- Final sensory claims come from the actual SKU. Affordable/local-made is an ambition until separately verified.
- Avoid “best,” “better tasting,” “healthier,” “freshest,” and sale/price language without the required facts and platform review.
- Preserve internal concept/provenance notes in the review interface. Customer artwork should contain customer copy; do not bake project notes into exported images.
