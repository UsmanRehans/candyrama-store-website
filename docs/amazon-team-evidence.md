# Avery: evidence for the Amazon creative team

Research date: September 13, 2026. Scope: official competitor product and merchandising pages, read today. These observations describe published creative and claims, not proven conversion, sales, or customer preference. No Amazon backend connection is required for this work. The latest Candy Rama direction is the pink-pouch peach campaign in `amazon-packaging-v4.md`; the rejected blue macro and earlier packaging-free direction are superseded.

## Four actionable findings

| Observed evidence | Implication for this draft | Hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Albanese names individual flavor/color pairs and describes a soft chew on its 12 Flavor Bears page. | Put one accurate taste/texture description beside the corresponding pouch or verified product close-up. Candy Rama's exact flavor mapping is still unconfirmed; do not borrow theirs. | Specific sensory information helps shoppers explain what they expect to receive more accurately than broad excitement copy. |
| Albanese explicitly qualifies that its small 1 oz bags may not include every flavor. | Treat assortment completeness and proportions as product facts. Keep the two pouch varieties distinguishable; do not turn a mixed campaign image into a promise that every item is in one bag. | An accurate package-to-contents relationship reduces misunderstanding about the purchased assortment. |
| SmartSweets places sour intensity, five named flavors, and quantified nutrition claims near its product title. | A concise hierarchy can communicate identity, experience, and substantiated facts. Candy Rama should use its sensory story and recognizable packaging; nutrition advantages require its own verified label evidence. | A short, legible message hierarchy improves recall on a narrow screen. |
| Sugarfina merchandises named candies alongside actual customizable Bento Box gift formats. | A gift position needs a corresponding product experience. Candy Rama's existing pouches support an approachable treat/sharing concept; avoid implying a gift box or upscale bundle that is not supplied. | An everyday occasion feels more credible for the current pouch offering than a luxury gifting narrative. |

Sources, all accessed September 13, 2026: [Albanese 12 Flavor Gummi Bears](https://www.albanesecandy.com/12-flavor-gummi-bears/), [SmartSweets Sour Blast Buddies](https://smartsweets.com/products/sour-blast-buddies), [Sugarfina bestsellers](https://www.sugarfina.com/candy/all-candy/best-sellers). ## Recommended sequence for Lauren and Vita

1. Recognizable pink pouches on peach: preserve the current identity and show the clear windows. Lead with a brief sensory invitation, not the rebrand story.
2. Product discovery: separate mixed gummies and fruit slices visually; do not name unverified flavors or imply both bags are a sold bundle.
3. Texture detail: use the exact approved product photo or an honestly labeled concept. Generated contents and tiny label text still need verification.
4. Everyday sharing: present an occasion without asserting one bag fills a large bowl, a serving count, or a price advantage.

This is an inference from category patterns and the owner brief, not evidence that these modules will sell better. Miles owns Amazon placement and policy validation. Lauren owns final wording. Cando resolves layout preferences against readability and product truth.

## What Avery can do before backend integration

Run competitor and category research, check product fact consistency, audit tracking plans, analyze manually supplied aggregate exports, and design practical tests. Do not build an Amazon data pipeline until there is a recurring decision that needs it. An initial export should identify marketplace, date range/timezone, product identifier, metric definitions, returns, discounts, and fees so revenue is not mistaken for profit.

For the current draft, conduct a small directional comprehension test with intended shoppers: briefly show the same-size creative, then ask what product/pack they expect, what flavor or texture they recall, and where they would click. Record exact response counts and recurring misunderstandings; preference is not conversion. Once live data and an eligible experimentation method are available, predefine a primary purchase or product-click measure, denominator, exposure window, and minimum useful effect. Hold SKU, price, promotions, and availability stable where possible. Before/after movement alone cannot establish that the images caused the change.

## Claims still blocked by missing evidence

Better tasting than competitors; cheaper or best value; locally manufactured or Texas-made; handmade; specific ingredients or nutrition advantages; certifications; definitive flavor counts; pack dimensions; current availability. Texas-based is recorded owner direction, distinct from manufacturing origin. This does not block a reviewable brand concept, but product-specific upload assets require approved packaging artwork and SKU facts.
