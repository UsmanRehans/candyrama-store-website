## Gummy A+ — four distinct jobs

### 1. Identity and appetite

**MIXED GUMMIES. MAIN CHARACTER ENERGY.**

A colorful cast for your candy stash.

Image: exact pink gummy-pouch identity with corresponding loose candy. The product name does the identification work; the second phrase provides brand voice.

### 2. Visual guide

**MEET THE MIX.**

Get closer to the colors, shapes, and finishes.

Three short detail labels: **Glossy surfaces.** / **Sugar-dusted pieces.** / **Mixed shapes.**

Use each label only when the selected approved image visibly supports it. Do not add flavor names, flavor counts, sour/sweet intensity, or chew/interior claims inferred from color or generated imagery. The guide is a visual concept rather than an approved assortment checklist; keep that qualification in reviewer mode. Distinct crops or actual pieces should show these differences clearly. Do not use the same pouch window three times.

### 3. Pack identity

**HERE’S THE GOOD STUFF.**

The pink pouch. The candy inside.

Use the full pouch with quiet labels **Candy Rama pouch** and **Clear candy window** only if useful. Do not invent a resealability, freshness, pack count, or piece-yield claim. Add a reviewer fact card: **Reference label: NET WT. 8 OZ. Observed on supplied photograph; final sold SKU and pack configuration not confirmed.**

Do not present a freestanding “8 oz per order” sales promise merely because the reference photograph has that weight. If the demo needs to show a proposed customer fact row, mark that row as a proposed reference-pack fact in reviewer mode, clearly separated from approved customer copy. The module remains incomplete as product information until that mapping is verified; decorative labels do not resolve this.

### 4. Human sharing

**PRESS PLAY. PASS THE CANDY.**

Find your spot. Bring the candy. Make a night of it.

Show a plausible sharing moment with matching candy. No implied serving count, two-pouch bundle, or exaggerated bowl yield. The invitation is the message; avoid an extra slogan underneath it.
