## Adopt, defer, avoid

**Adopt first:** readable SKU facts; contextual shipping/returns/allergen links; accessible FAQ structure; clear support routing; thoughtful footer grouping; real product discovery within the existing catalog. Preserve direct ordering and unpaid-order review behavior already authorized for Candy Rama.

**Build trust modules as a priority, with content gates:** customer stories, ratings and creator videos are an important part of the requested richer storefront. Build the components alongside the core discovery work; publish individual assets only with authentic Candy Rama relevance, permission, attribution and product mapping. Require captions, pause/reduced-motion support and sensible loading. Content evidence gates publication, not component development. Defer additional marketplace buttons until exact approved product listings exist; do not build an Amazon backend integration. Defer sparse new collections and unsupported sale/popularity labels.

**Avoid:** competitor colors/layout/assets/copy; marketplace-style shipping promises on Candy Rama PDPs; unsupported verified-purchase badges; empty promotional collections; copying legal terms; generic social-root links; representing marketplace-store links as a specific SKU purchase; relying on initial empty HTML as a product-loading strategy.

## Measurement for later implementation

Record product-list view, product click, help-topic open, successful cart addition, checkout start, unpaid order request submitted, payment confirmed, and support submission as distinct events. Do not count an unpaid request as revenue. Baseline mobile and desktop funnels before release; compare the same date windows and traffic mix afterward. Measure product discovery, completion errors and repetitive support questions; do not promise a conversion lift from this audit.
