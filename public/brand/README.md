## Earlier logo suite

The `candyrama-2a`, `candyrama-2b`, and `candyrama-2c` files preserve reversed logo variants. Avatar exports are available at 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 pixels. Oval stamp, sticker PNG/SVG exports and the earlier print preview are included as historical brand assets.

These variants are archived for reference; inclusion does not designate them all as approved primary marks. Historical slogans, establishment years and origin wording should be checked before reuse. Existing `candyrama-wordmark.png` and `amazon-preview-logo.png` remain available.
